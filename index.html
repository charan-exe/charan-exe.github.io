The idea

Learning technology by writing about it.

Technology changes quickly. Instead of simply reading about new technologies and moving on, I like to understand them, research them and explain what I learn through writing.

This website is a collection of those ideas, explanations, discoveries and observations across electronics, automation, AI and other emerging technologies.

About the Writer

Curious about how technology works.

I'm Charan Teja, a technology writer interested in understanding how modern technologies work and how they are changing the world around us.

My writing covers electronics, embedded systems, industrial automation, artificial intelligence, automotive technology and other emerging areas.

I don't write to present myself as an expert in everything I cover. I write to learn, research, understand and share useful explanations with students, beginners and technology enthusiasts.
